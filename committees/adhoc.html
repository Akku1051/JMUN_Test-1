Crisis Committee

AdHoc

A sealed crisis experience where the premise stays hidden until delegates are already inside the room.

Committee Focus

Expect the unexpected. The AdHoc Committee challenges delegates to think on their feet, adapt to rapidly evolving situations, and respond to developments that no one could have fully anticipated. With limited preparation and an unpredictable agenda, delegates must rely on creativity, strategic thinking, and quick decision-making to navigate whatever unfolds. In a committee where the next crisis could change everything, adaptability may be the most valuable skill in the room.

Topic will be revealed on the day of the committee.

Your hint: What lengths would you go to restore reality?
