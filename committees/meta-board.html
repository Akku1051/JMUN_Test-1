Meta Board of Directors

Delegates act as directors, executives, shareholders, and regulators shaping the strategy of the company behind Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, and more.

Committee Focus

Welcome to the boardroom. As members of Meta’s Board of Directors, delegates will step into the world of corporate governance, where technological innovation, public accountability, and financial interests collide. From navigating ethical controversies to managing competitive pressures and shaping the future of social media, every decision carries consequences for the company and the wider world. Delegates must think like business leaders, weigh competing stakeholder interests, and determine how Meta should navigate an increasingly complex digital landscape.
