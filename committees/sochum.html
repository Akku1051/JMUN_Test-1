Social, Cultural and Humanitarian Committee

SOCHUM examines human rights questions through culture, labor, equity, and international responsibility.

Committee Focus

The Social, Humanitarian and Cultural Committee (SOCHUM) addresses some of the most pressing humanitarian and social challenges facing the international community. Delegates will work together to debate complex global issues, develop collaborative solutions, and balance national interests with the pursuit of human rights and international cooperation. Through diplomacy, negotiation, and thoughtful debate, delegates will explore how the international community can build a more equitable and secure future.

Topics

  • Offshoring Labor and Slavery
