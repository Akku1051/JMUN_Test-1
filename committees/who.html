Specialized Committee

World Health Organization

WHO connects nations, partners, and individuals to promote health and coordinate global responses to health emergencies.

Committee Focus

The World Health Organization works to promote global health, coordinate international responses to public health threats, and improve access to essential healthcare. Delegates in WHO will confront complex health challenges that transcend national borders, requiring scientific understanding, international cooperation, and practical policymaking. From emerging infectious diseases to antimicrobial resistance, delegates must develop effective strategies that balance public health priorities, national capabilities, and the needs of vulnerable populations.

Topics

  • Determining and Mitigating the Root Causes of Viral Outbreaks and Pandemics Worldwide
  • Addressing Antimicrobial Resistance Resulting from the Misuse and Overuse of Antibiotics
