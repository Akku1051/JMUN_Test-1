UN Security Council

The Security Council is responsible for maintaining international peace and security.

Committee Focus

The United Nations Security Council is tasked with maintaining international peace and security, confronting global conflicts, and responding to threats to international stability. In this committee, delegates will navigate high-stakes geopolitical tensions, negotiate under pressure, and make difficult decisions with far-reaching consequences. With national interests at odds and the international order at stake, delegates must demonstrate strategic thinking, persuasive diplomacy, and a willingness to compromise to achieve meaningful outcomes.

Topics

  • Global Nuclear Stability & the Risk of Escalation between Nuclear-Armed States
  • Protection of Civilians in Armed Conflict
