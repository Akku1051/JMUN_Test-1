The Invitation

Welcome to JMUN 2026.

A letter from Akshat Shukla, Secretary-General of Saint Joseph Model United Nations 2026.

Secretary-General Invitation

Dear Delegates, Advisors, and Distinguished Guests,

On behalf of the Saint Joseph Model United Nations Secretariat, it is my distinct pleasure to welcome you to JMUN 2026.

As Secretary General, I am incredibly excited to have the pleasure to invite you to join us this November for a conference built on diplomacy, critical thinking and meaningful debate. JMUN is more than a Model UN conference, it is an opportunity for students to challenge their perspectives, develop leadership skills and collaborate with peers from across the region to tackle some of the world's most pressing issues.

This year, our Secretariat has worked tirelessly to create engaging that will challenge delegates of all experience levels. Whether you are attending your first conference or are a seasoned veteran, you will find opportunities to fruitfully debate, negotiate, and leave a lasting impact on your committee. From traditional general assemblies to dynamic crisis simulations, delegates will be encouraged to think creatively, communicate effectively, and embrace the spirit of international cooperation.

At its core, Model UN demonstrates that progress is achieved, not through conflict, but through conversation. In an increasingly interconnected world, the ability to understand diverse perspectives and work towards common goals has never been more important. We hope that JMUN will serve as a platform for students to develop these skills while creating unforgettable memories and friendships along the way.

I would like to extend my sincere gratitude to our dedicated Secretariat team, staff, advisors and volunteers whose hard work made this conference possible. Their commitment and passion are what transformed JMUN from an idea into an exceptional experience for every delegate.

We are honoured that you have chosen to be part of JMUN 2026, and we look forward to welcoming you this November.

Best Regards,

Akshat Shukla
Secretary-General
