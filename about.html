About JMUN

A conference built for thoughtful delegates.

JMUN 2026 brings students together at St. Joseph Secondary School to practice diplomacy, public speaking, research, negotiation, and collaborative problem-solving.

Conference experience

Debate with purpose, learn with confidence.

Diplomacy

Represent perspectives

Delegates step into the role of countries, organizations, and decision-makers while learning to listen carefully and argue constructively.

Debate

Build solutions

Committees challenge students to research global issues, form blocs, draft resolutions, and respond to evolving committee priorities.

Community

Grow as leaders

JMUN is designed to welcome new delegates while giving experienced students meaningful room to lead, negotiate, and mentor peers.

Hosted by St. Joseph SS

Student-led from planning to closing ceremonies.

The JMUN Secretariat organizes the conference program, committee preparation, delegate experience, logistics, marketing, and crisis design. Together, the team is building a November conference centered on meaningful debate and a welcoming Model UN community.
